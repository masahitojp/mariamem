# Buffer-pool startup load OFF: ordinary measurement repeat

**Conclusion:** The same binary again shows fewer long COUNTs with OFF and lower median paired whole-batch time, while P8 Fork latency again exceeds the unchanged 5% regression guard. The fixed experiment ends as a throughput/latency tradeoff, not automatic adoption or permanent Issue68 resolution.

## Original vs repeat: keep cohorts separate

Original report source_commit `11cdb5df4f199af177348b7015b68296e94a4f31`, branch `experiment/buffer-load-off`, keeps its original receipts. This is a new observation set with the same measured binary. No pooled incidence or source relabelling. Positive savings means OFF lower/faster.

| Cohort | P | Median paired batch saved | CPU saved | Fork saved | Batch wins /5 | COUNT >100 ON → OFF | OFF UPDATE max ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| original | 4 | 9.07% | 3.92% | -0.02% | 5/5 | 2 → 0 | 4.17 |
| original | 8 | 33.26% | 5.27% | -18.33% | 5/5 | 6 → 0 | 366.44 |
| repeat | 4 | 9.64% | 5.26% | -0.61% | 5/5 | 2 → 0 | 10.98 |
| repeat | 8 | 16.11% | 5.16% | -12.92% | 4/5 | 4 → 0 | 22.62 |

The direction of COUNT-tail reduction, whole-batch savings and the P8 Fork tradeoff repeats. P8 is no longer faster in every pair: round0 OFF batch864.3ms versus ON690.7ms. Median paired savings are16.11%, rather than the original33.26%; the size of the benefit is variable. Absolute timings in both arms are higher than the original cohort despite identical binary and hardware. Host load, thermal/scheduling and other environmental contributions were not isolated, so no causal claim or direct cross-cohort speed regression is made. Pairing controls time-local conditions without proving they are identical.

OFF has no >100ms COUNT, UPDATE, check SELECT or combined SQL in this cohort; its P4/P8 UPDATE maxima are10.98/22.62ms. The previous366ms UPDATE and six diagnostic processes without reproduction remain valid separate observations. Absence here does not resolve that residual or prove a population rate.

P4 passes the original gates; P8 again fails the >5% Fork guard (median paired Fork+12.92%, +9.10ms). Thus reducer disposition remains `STOP_INCONCLUSIVE_OR_TRADEOFF`. Do not change thresholds after observing results. An explicit Human throughput-versus-Fork-latency decision and later canonical/platform work would be required before adopting OFF. No extra repetitions are authorized by this report.
